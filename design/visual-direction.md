# Visual Direction — Resonant Confidence

Status: **EXPLORATORY / NON-NORMATIVE**

## Intent

Belcanto should feel like a person is entering a living artistic community and
seeing evidence of their own transformation. It should not feel like opening a
school timetable, CRM, bank, marketplace, or generic luxury app.

## Visual grammar

- Dark-first ink canvas with quiet layer separation.
- Vivid violet for the main action path.
- Semantic accents used sparingly and consistently.
- Human photography and authentic performance moments provide emotion.
- Thin borders are preferred over heavy shadows.
- Rounded geometry is restrained: large radii for major surfaces, smaller
  radii for controls, pills only for compact statuses.
- Dense information is grouped into meaningful moments with clear hierarchy.

## Content hierarchy

1. The person's next meaningful action.
2. Evidence of growth.
3. Community and creative opportunities.
4. Administrative details.

## Motion

- `quick` 120 ms: pressed/focus response.
- `standard` 200 ms: state changes.
- `expressive` 320 ms: card/sheet reveal.
- `celebratory` 480 ms: earned milestone only.

Motion must support reduced-motion settings. No permanent pulsing, gratuitous
glow, parallax, or decorative animation loops.

## Explicit exclusions

- AI-looking gradients.
- Stock “luxury” black-and-gold styling.
- Decorative flourishes without meaning.
- Literal microphones, notes, piano keys, sound waves, vinyl, theatre masks,
  monograms, or generic geometric marks as identity.
- Dashboard walls, banking cards, CRM tables, and school-diary framing.
